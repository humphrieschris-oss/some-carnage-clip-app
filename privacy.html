Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Some Carnage Clip App handles information.

Information the App Accesses

  • Video clips selected or automatically created on the user's computer.
  • TikTok account authorization information required to upload content.
  • Basic TikTok account information made available through permissions authorized by the user.

How Information Is Used

  • Processing locally created gaming clips.
  • Converting clips into a format suitable for TikTok.
  • Authenticating the user's TikTok account.
  • Uploading authorized videos to the user's TikTok account.

Video Files

Video files are processed on the user's computer unless they are being transmitted directly to TikTok for upload.

The Some Carnage Clip App does not operate an independent cloud storage service for user videos.

Authentication Data

The app may store authorization tokens needed to communicate with TikTok. These tokens are used only for authorized TikTok API functions.

The app does not collect or store the user's TikTok password.

Sharing of Information

The app does not sell personal information.

Information is only transmitted to TikTok when required to perform authorized account or content-upload functions.

Data Retention

Local video clips remain on the user's computer until the user deletes them.

Authorization information may remain stored locally until the user revokes access or removes the app's stored credentials.

User Control

Users may stop using the app at any time and may revoke TikTok access through their TikTok account settings.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or data-handling practices change.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the developer of the Some Carnage Clip App.
